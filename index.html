Tiny Cat: Cute Watch Face

Privacy policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026
Developer: Muneef M
Android package: com.petwatchface.tinycat

The Tiny Cat watch-face package does not send personal data to the developer. Your watch renders its artwork, time and selected data locally. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send.

What this policy covers

This policy describes Tiny Cat: Cute Watch Face for Wear OS, including its kitten, puppy and bunny designs. It also explains how support correspondence and visits to this policy page are handled.

Information on your watch

Tiny Cat contains bundled artwork and a Watch Face Format layout. It does not include developer-operated servers, user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or network code.

Wear OS uses local time, date and your customization choices to display the face. Pet, background, color and pose choices are managed by your watch. Tiny Cat does not send these choices to us.

The two optional data slots, called complications, display information supplied by providers you select, such as steps or watch battery. The operating system and those providers manage their data, permissions and connectivity under their own policies. Tiny Cat does not independently upload the displayed values or maintain a developer-hosted activity history.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles purchasing, payment and installation under Google's policies. Tiny Cat does not process or receive your payment-card number. Google may make order-related information available to the developer for purchase support, refunds or required recordkeeping.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's services.

Contacting support

If you email mailmuneef@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve issues. Our email service provider, Google, processes that correspondence as part of delivering email.

We do not sell personal information or use support correspondence for advertising. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or to service providers involved in handling your request.

Data security

Watch-face information remains subject to your device's security and storage protections. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider's security controls; we do not operate a separate database of watch-face users. This policy page is served over HTTPS. No method of storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

There is no Tiny Cat account or developer-hosted watch-face profile to delete. You can change local customization choices in your watch's editor or uninstall the face using your watch's controls.

Support correspondence and any order-related records are retained only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. To request deletion of information you sent us, contact mailmuneef@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required by law. Google and complication providers handle deletion of information held by their own services.

This privacy-policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about page requests, such as IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics, advertising, forms or tracking cookies to this page.

Changes to this policy

If the watch face's data handling changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Any new data collection will be described before or when the relevant functionality is introduced.
