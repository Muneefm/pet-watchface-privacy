Rainy Ride: Car Watch Face

Privacy policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Developer: Muneef M
Android package: com.watchface.rainyride

The Rainy Ride watch face does not send personal data to the developer. Its artwork, time, date, step display and selected car are rendered on your watch. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Rainy Ride: Car Watch Face app for Wear OS, support correspondence, and visits to this policy page.

Information on your watch

Rainy Ride is a resource-only Watch Face Format package. It includes no developer-operated server, account, advertising, analytics SDK, or network code.

Wear OS supplies the local time, date and step count shown on the face. Your watch stores the car choice and renders the short rain animation locally. Rainy Ride does not independently upload these values or keep a developer-hosted activity history. The watch operating system manages step data and its associated settings.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles purchasing, payment and installation under Google's policies. Rainy Ride does not process or receive your payment-card number. Google may make order-related information available to the developer for purchase support, refunds or required recordkeeping. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

If you email mailmuneef@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Google processes the correspondence as our email provider. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to providers helping us respond.

Retention and deletion

There is no Rainy Ride account or developer-hosted watch-face profile to delete. You can change the local car choice in the watch editor or uninstall the face. Support messages and order-related records are kept only as needed to respond and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. To request deletion of information sent to us, email mailmuneef@gmail.com. Google handles deletion of information held in its own services.

This page and updates

This page is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics, ads, forms or tracking cookies to this page. We will update this policy and its effective date if Rainy Ride's data handling changes.
