Snow Club Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy covers Snow Club, the Wear OS watch face with package name com.watchface.snowclub. For questions, contact mailmuneef@gmail.com.

Information used on your watch

Snow Club displays your watch's time, local date and battery level. It calculates the days until Christmas on the watch. Your visual preferences are managed through the Wear OS watch face editor.

Data collection and sharing

The watch face does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking tools or an account system. It works offline after installation.

Purchases and platform services

Google Play handles the purchase, installation and any applicable refund. Google and your device provider operate their services under their own privacy policies. Snow Club does not receive your payment card details.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting the same email address.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the watch face or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
